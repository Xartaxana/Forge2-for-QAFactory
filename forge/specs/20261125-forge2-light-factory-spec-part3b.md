  - `stale`: relaunch with the stale rows only.
  - `shortfall`: relaunch the producer with those rows only; the consumer's dependent rows `held`.
  - `no_move`: conductor seam.
- Relaunch block (via `context.py --relaunch`): cause, round, the ids, the sections they name whole, the answer verbatim; never a previous transcript or summary (AR-21, P4).
